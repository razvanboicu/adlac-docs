Theme section

Blog

Display a Shopify blog listing.

ADLAC 1.0.0Shopify Online Store 2.0
Where to configure thisThis is a template or internal section and may not be listed in Add section. Open its applicable template or related feature.

What this section does

Configure the blog template and featured article presentation.

How to set it up

  1. Open your Shopify theme editor and select the appropriate template.
  2. Configure the section settings in the tables below.
  3. Preview using your store content.
  4. Check the result on desktop and mobile, then save.
