What ADLAC includes

  • Header section group with announcement bar, search, customer account controls, mega menus, and compact dropdowns.
  • Footer section group with newsletter, menus, social links, Follow on Shop, payment icons, and localization selectors.
  • Product media for images, Shopify-hosted video, external video, and 3D models.
  • Variant options with Shopify swatches and linked-product option URLs.
  • Quantity rules, pickup availability, selling plans, gift card recipient fields, accelerated checkout, and payment terms.
  • Nested product information tabs that accept Description, Product specifications, Custom Liquid, and app blocks.
  • Related and complementary product recommendations.
  • Collection and search filtering, sorting, active filter chips, mobile filter drawers, and pagination.
  • SEO metadata, Open Graph/Twitter metadata, and Shopify product structured data.
