Quick Buy

Use ADLAC Quick Buy for fast variant selection and cart actions while preserving full product pages for more complex purchase flows.

ADLAC 1.0.0Shopify Online Store 2.0
Quick Buy is intentionally selective.Product cards open the Quick Buy dialog for products that can be configured safely in the dialog. Gift cards and products with selling plans route customers to the full product page instead.

Supported media

  • Product images.
  • Shopify-hosted product video with native controls.
  • External product video such as YouTube or Vimeo.
  • 3D models using Shopify's model viewer.

When a selected variant has featured media, that media is presented first. Customers can move through media with previous/next controls and a media counter.

Product options

The dialog renders variant option controls, including Shopify swatches, unavailable option states, and linked-product option URLs. The selected variant updates the product state and media.

Quantity and cart

The quantity field uses the selected variant's minimum, maximum, and increment rules. Add to cart uses the theme's AJAX cart behavior and updates the cart experience without replacing the compact icon button with long loading text.

Global Quick Buy appearance

  • Product image fit: show the entire media or fill the media area.
  • Media ratio: adapt, square, portrait, or landscape.
  • Product image position: center, top, or bottom.
  • Product image corner radius and spacing.
  • Optional product image border.
  • Media frame style: Clean, Soft card, or Glass.
  • Optional ambient image backdrop.
