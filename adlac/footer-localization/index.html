ADLAC / Footer & localization

Store footer

Footer & localization

Configure newsletter, navigation, social links, payment icons, Follow on Shop, and language/country selectors.

ADLAC 1.0.0Shopify Online Store 2.0

Footer content

The Footer can render a newsletter signup, footer navigation menu, social links, Follow on Shop, enabled payment method icons, copyright/powered-by content, and Shopify localization controls.

Newsletter

The newsletter uses Shopify's customer form and tags submitted contacts as newsletter. Success and error messages are rendered by the theme.

Social links

Add Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, and X URLs in Theme settings → Social media. The footer only renders networks that have a URL.

Follow on Shop

If the store supports Shopify's Follow on Shop feature and the Footer setting is enabled, the theme renders Shopify's native follow control.

Language and country selectors

When more than one language or country is available, the footer renders language and country/currency selectors using Shopify's localization form. Country changes preserve the current storefront path while allowing Shopify to apply the selected market context.

Payment icons

When enabled, ADLAC renders icons for the payment types enabled on the store.
