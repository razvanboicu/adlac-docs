ADLAC / Theme settings

Global configuration

Theme settings

These settings apply across the storefront and are available from the Theme settings panel.

ADLAC 1.0.0Shopify Online Store 2.0

Recommended order

Start with Typography and Colors, then set Layout and Style. Configure Quick buy only after your product media is representative of the catalog.

Quick Buy theme settings

The Quick Buy settings are global. They control image/media fit, ratio, position, corner radius, spacing, border, frame style, and the ambient backdrop across Quick Buy dialogs.
