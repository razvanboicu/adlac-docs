ADLAC 1.0.0

Initial Theme Store release candidate.

  • Flexible Shopify Online Store 2.0 sections and theme blocks.
  • Rich product media and configurable product gallery.
  • Product variant swatches, quantity rules, pickup availability, selling plans, gift cards, accelerated checkout, and payment terms.
  • Nested product information tabs with app block support.
  • Technical specifications from optional specs.* product metafields.
  • Quick Buy with image, video, external video, and 3D model media.
  • Collection/search filtering, sorting, product cards, and variant image previews.
  • Header mega menus, visual menus, compact dropdowns, predictive search, and rotating search text.
  • Shopify localization selectors, newsletter, social links, Follow on Shop, and payment icons.
