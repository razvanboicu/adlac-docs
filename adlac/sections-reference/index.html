Section reference

All Shopify Theme Editor controls, defaults, and blocks, available in the ADLAC Theme Editor.

ADLAC 1.0.0Shopify Online Store 2.0ADLAC 1.0.0 settings

Setting labels and defaults are resolved from sections/*.liquid and locales/en.default.schema.json. Shopify may conditionally hide settings depending on the editor state.
