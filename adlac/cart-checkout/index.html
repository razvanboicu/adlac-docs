Cart & checkout

Understand the ADLAC cart drawer, cart page, quantity updates, notes, selling-plan context, and accelerated checkout controls.

ADLAC 1.0.0Shopify Online Store 2.0

Cart drawer

The cart drawer is rendered globally and can show a cart note. It supports line-item updates and is refreshed by the theme's AJAX cart behavior after eligible add-to-cart actions.

Cart page

The Cart template supports line item quantities, vendor display, cart notes, pricing summaries, selling-plan information when present, and checkout actions.

Discounts and selling plans

ADLAC is designed around Shopify cart and product objects, so automatic discounts and selling-plan details can flow through the cart without provider-specific theme code.

Accelerated checkout

The Product Buy buttons block can show Shopify accelerated checkout. Merchants can control button height, whether it matches the theme button radius, a custom radius, and an optional subtle shadow.

Payment terms

When enabled and supported by the product/payment configuration, the Buy buttons block renders Shopify payment terms/installment messaging.
