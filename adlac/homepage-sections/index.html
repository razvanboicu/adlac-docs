Homepage & sections

Choose sections by the job they need to do: product discovery, collection discovery, media storytelling, editorial content, or conversion.

ADLAC 1.0.0Shopify Online Store 2.0

Product and collection storytelling

Product mosaic

Floating mosaic, equal grid, or horizontal rail layouts with optional product info, Quick Buy, swatches, glass styling, and mobile carousel/grid/stack layouts.

Product stage

One product at a time with product blocks, transitions, autoplay, tabs/arrows/pause controls, image framing, and optional product specifications.

Pre-order + accessories

Pairs a featured product/story panel with accessory product cards, responsive accessory layouts, Quick Add, and customizable glass/theme colors.

Collection showcase

Editorial collection cards with multiple desktop/mobile layouts, descriptions, product counts, hover effects, and configurable imagery.

Media-led sections

Immersive media banner

Image, slideshow, or Shopify-hosted video hero with mobile media, overlays, glass transitions, media motion, adaptive glow, responsive layout, and content blocks.

Shot on product

Combines a product story/card with Shopify-hosted or external video, product image/price, configurable media position, aspect ratio, glow, and specification blocks.

Video story

Shopify-hosted or external video with full-media or split layouts, cover image, playback behavior, aspect ratio, and content blocks.

Slideshow

Responsive image slides with mobile images, arrows/dots, autoplay, slide transitions, optional image effects, overlays, and CTAs.

Editorial and utility sections

ADLAC also includes Image with text, Rich text, Multicolumn, Collapsible content, Newsletter, Featured collection, Featured product, Collection list, Blog posts, Custom Liquid, and a generic Section wrapper for theme/app blocks.

For every exact setting and default value, use the Section reference.
