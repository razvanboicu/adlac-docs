Content templates

Blog & pages

Configure editorial content, standard pages, the contact page, password page, collection list, and 404 recovery.

ADLAC 1.0.0Shopify Online Store 2.0

Blog and article templates

The Blog template supports an eyebrow, description, configurable articles per page, desktop columns, image ratio, author display, excerpts, excerpt length, and tags. The Article template supports the featured image and article content, and the runtime locale includes comment and pagination messaging.

Pages

The default Page template can show or hide the page title and control content width. The Contact page uses the Contact form section with configurable content width and an optional phone field.

Collection list

The List collections template provides a grid of collections with configurable heading/intro, product counts, descriptions, image ratio/fit, desktop/mobile columns, spacing, and pagination.

Password and 404

The Password template supports a logo, logo width, eyebrow, and footer text. The 404 template can optionally show search to help customers recover from a missing URL.
